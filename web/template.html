The editorial filter

Useful beats loud.

  • Upstream: crawling, indexes, retrieval, extraction, connectors and grounded-answer quality, pricing or access changes with web context.
  • Downstream: research, monitoring, enrichment and multi-step applications using external web information. Relevant independent analysis and implementations qualify too.
  • Thin-teaser first-party launches are labeled Agent products, not confirmed web-search capabilities. Generic AI, SEO and internal-document-only RAG are not enough.
  • Use publisher timestamps within a rolling seven-day window; reject future and undated entries.
  • No article scraping, paywall workarounds or paid AI. A small hosted CPU model rewrites public feed evidence or qualified seed notes; a rewrite is not independent verification or a full-article review.
